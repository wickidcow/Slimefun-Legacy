# Slimefun Legacy 4.1.59

Slimefun Legacy 4.1.59 turns the Guide's former Doctor/placeholder area into a focused **Slimefun Recovery Center** for operators and administrators, with clearer resource-pack recovery workflows and stronger safety boundaries.

## Admin-only Slimefun Recovery Center

The Guide now exposes one clear **Slimefun Recovery Center** instead of overlapping Doctor/placeholder actions.

- Access requires `slimefun.command.doctor` (operator by default).
- Normal players do not see the Recovery Center button.
- Package-visible Recovery Center submenus repeat the permission check so another guide integration cannot bypass the admin boundary.
- JEG and the classic Slimefun Guide route into the same native Recovery Center when Slimefun Legacy is present.
- The main dashboard separates Resource Pack & Item Textures, Player & Item Repair, Addon & Dependency Health, Runtime & Storage Health, Advanced System Health, Additional Recovery Tools, and Support & Diagnostics.
- The Recovery Assistant routes administrators to the safest relevant lane instead of performing mutations automatically.

## Resource Pack & Item Textures

Resource-pack delivery, item-model mappings, and stored-item texture repair are now deliberately presented as separate operations.

### Pack sender controls

**Enable Legacy Pack Sender** and **Disable Legacy Pack Sender** now require confirmation.

Before confirming, the UI shows the exact ownership transition that will occur. Enabling the Legacy sender is blocked when the configured pack URL/SHA-1 preflight is invalid instead of enabling a sender that cannot safely deliver its pack.

Sender controls do not alter `item-models.yml` or stored Slimefun item texture metadata.

Servers using ItemsAdder, Oraxen, a proxy/server pack, or another combined-pack owner can continue running with the Legacy sender disabled while retaining matching Slimefun model mappings.

### Upgrade Items for Resource Pack

Pack adoption is now a guided:

1. **Scan Pack Upgrade** — read-only audit.
2. **Apply Pack Texture Upgrade** — confirmed mutation after review and backup.

Only zero-valued Legacy mappings are eligible for bundled-model adoption. Custom non-zero mappings remain protected.

### Remove Resource-Pack Item Models

Removal is now a guided:

1. **Audit Model Removal** — read-only audit.
2. **Remove Legacy Item Models** — confirmed removal of exact Legacy bundled mappings only.

The UI explicitly states that this **does not unregister Slimefun items or machines**. If there are no exact Legacy mappings to remove, no destructive removal action is offered.

### Resource Pack Item Texture Repairs

Stored-item repair is a separate lane from mapping removal.

- The menu shows the latest completed Doctor item-model snapshot.
- `/sf doctor item-models scan` remains read-only.
- `/sf doctor item-models repair confirm` remains a separate confirmed repair.
- Custom/non-matching model data stays protected.
- The workflow tells operators to review chat output, reopen the menu, and only then proceed.

## Cleaner system health and support views

**Advanced System Health** replaces the older Operations Center wording and retains read-mostly health lanes for items, storage, machines, addons, proxy state, resource-pack state, performance, and upgrade readiness.

The Support & Diagnostics summary now also identifies the active guide provider so an operator can immediately see whether the native guide or maintained JEG owns the current enhanced guide UI.

Runtime and resource-pack cards use live attention states so healthy status does not look equivalent to an active recovery problem.

## SF_JustEnoughGuide 2.1.64

The maintained JEG companion release is **2.1.64**.

Its former `U` unknown-feature slot is now semantically the **Recovery Center** slot, uses the Totem of Undying entry, is hidden from non-admin players, and prefers Slimefun Legacy's native Recovery Center. The old internal placeholder constant remains as a deprecated source-compatibility alias.

The canonical addon bundle is pinned to the released JEG 2.1.64 source commit.

## Safety and compatibility

4.1.59 does not introduce a new repair engine, automatic item-model cleanup, automatic addon migration, storage schema change, or normal gameplay/Cargo/Energy behavior change.

Regression verification protects:

- OP/admin-only Recovery Center visibility and entry points;
- sender ownership reconciliation and confirmation;
- pack configuration preflight before sender enablement;
- scan/audit-before-mutation resource-pack flows;
- exact Legacy mapping removal boundaries;
- separation of mapping removal from stored-item texture repair;
- preservation of custom/non-matching model metadata;
- maintained JEG Recovery Center integration.

The maintained compatibility policy remains unchanged: Paper 26.2 is the primary production line, Paper/Purpur 1.21.11 remain supported compatibility-floor targets, and Paper 26.3 remains under candidate/full-stack validation.

4.1.59 uses exact released 4.1.58 commit `2d7343060073cc0b7ce5ca462935ea3250b76f65` as its release-blocking previous-stable baseline.

The canonical release publishes `Slimefun-Legacy4.1.59.jar` plus the validated `SF_Addons_1.21.11-26.3.zip` bundle.
