# Slimefun Legacy 4.1.50 — Built-in Addons & Guide Parity

Slimefun Legacy 4.1.50 continues the effort to preserve classic Slimefun and addon behavior while making older content feel native on current Minecraft versions.

## Built-in ExtraGear and ExtraTools

ExtraGear and ExtraTools are now maintained directly inside Slimefun Legacy as optional internal addons. Both are enabled by default through `configSFLAddons.yml`, while duplicate protection prevents the built-in copy from registering when a standalone plugin with the same addon name is installed.

### Dedicated Slimefun Guide pages

The bundled addons now match the original standalone guide experience again:

- **ExtraGear** has its own top-level **ExtraGear** page containing its swords and armor.
- **Extra Tools** has its own top-level **Extra Tools** page containing the Hammer and ExtraTools machines.
- ExtraGear content is no longer mixed into Slimefun's core Weapons or Armor pages.

The original ExtraGear `extragear:items` group and ExtraTools `extratools:extra_tools` group identities are preserved.

## Compatibility preservation

The built-in imports preserve the historical contracts players and worlds depend on:

- legacy Slimefun item IDs
- research namespaces and research IDs
- recipes
- enchantments
- machine behavior and inventories
- saved-world and stored-item compatibility

Regression checks now protect both the addon import contracts and their dedicated guide-page placement.

## ExtraTools correction

The ExtraTools Cobblestone Generator tooltip now reflects its real machine behavior: a **512 J power buffer** and approximately **32 J/s** average consumption. This is a display correction only; its production rate and energy mechanics are unchanged.

## Addon bundle

The refreshed `SFL_AddonsNDependencies.zip` is rebuilt from current maintained addon releases and dependencies. Standalone ExtraTools and ExtraGear JARs are intentionally excluded because those addons are now available directly inside Slimefun Legacy.

## Server compatibility

The changes in this release were validated through the Slimefun Legacy CI matrix, including:

- Paper 26.2
- Purpur 26.2
- Folia 26.2
- Leaf 26.2
- Paper/Purpur 1.21.11 compatibility
- Paper 26.3 pre-release preflight
- Public API compatibility
- Slimefun addon compatibility probes

## Upgrading

If your server currently uses standalone ExtraGear or ExtraTools, remove those standalone JARs when moving to the built-in versions. The internal modules are enabled by default. Servers that deliberately want to keep a standalone copy can do so; Slimefun Legacy detects it and skips registration of the corresponding built-in module.
