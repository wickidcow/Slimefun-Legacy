# Irradiated Arsenal

The **Irradiated Arsenal** is a combat branch of Adventurer's Curios built around late-game Blistering Ingot III infusion.

## Weapons

| Weapon | Base item | Exposure on hit | Extra damage |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Glowbite Blade | Netherite Sword | 10 | +1.0 |
| Geiger Pike | Netherite Spear | 8 | +0.75 |
| Hot-Zone Cleaver | Netherite Axe | 12 | +1.25 |
| Reactor's Fork | Trident | 9 | +1.0 |
| Critical Mass | Mace | 16 | +2.0 |
| Radspike Arrow | Arrow | 7 | +0.5 |

All five reusable weapons are made in the Magic Workbench by surrounding the vanilla weapon with eight **Blistering Ingot III**. Eight Radspike Arrows are infused from eight arrows and four Blistering Ingot III.

## Radiation rules

- Irradiated weapons use Slimefun's existing radiation exposure system.
- Merely storing one in a normal inventory slot does not leak radiation.
- Holding one in either hand applies a low passive dose at a deliberately slow interval.
- Successfully damaging something with an irradiated weapon gives the wielder a very small self-dose.
- Player targets receive the weapon-specific exposure shown above.
- Non-player living targets receive the weapon's additional radiation-themed damage.
- Full Slimefun radiation-protection armor blocks exposure for both wielder and target.
- Creative and spectator players do not gain exposure.
- Radspike Arrows preserve their custom identity when fired from bows/crossbows so the radiation payload follows the projectile.

This family intentionally uses original retro-wasteland flavor rather than copying text or names from another game franchise.
