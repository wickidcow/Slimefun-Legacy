# Slimefun Legacy 4.1.46 — Arachnid Ward Torch

The Arachnid Ward Torch is a new Adventurer's Curios item for cave exploration.

- Held in either hand: creates an 8-block mobile spider ward.
- Placed as a torch: creates a 12-block stationary ward.
- Spiders and Cave Spiders drop player targets inside the protected area and are nudged away from the ward instead of being damaged.
- Held and placed protection do not stack; the strongest applicable ward wins.
- Spider jockeys are naturally displaced because the spider mount is repelled.
- The implementation is local and bounded: no global entity scans and no chunk force-loading.
- Defaults are configurable in `configSFLAddons.yml`.

The item remains part of Slimefun Legacy core under Adventurer's Curios and does not require another addon.
