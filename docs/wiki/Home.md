Welcome to the Slimefun Legacy Wiki. This documentation keeps the familiar organization of the classic Slimefun 4 community wiki while updating the project-specific guidance for Slimefun Legacy, current Paper servers, the Enhanced Guide, compatibility tooling, production safeguards and diagnostics.

Legacy-maintained pages take precedence. Classic item and gameplay pages that have not needed a Legacy rewrite are inherited from the original community wiki at a pinned revision and are clearly attributed under the MIT license.

## Current platform target

Slimefun Legacy is maintained primarily for **Paper 26.2 / Minecraft 26.2 on Java 25**. Purpur based on the same Paper line is supported. Folia remains an **experimental** secondary target, and every installed addon must also be Folia-safe.

For the exact current build, use the repository's Releases page rather than relying on a version number copied into documentation.

## Wiki accuracy

For recipes and item availability on a particular server, **the in-game Slimefun Guide is authoritative**. Server owners can disable content, configuration can alter behavior, and addons can add or modify categories.

Pages inherited from the historical Slimefun 4 wiki are reference material. If an inherited page conflicts with a Legacy-maintained page, current in-game behavior, or current Slimefun Legacy release notes, the Legacy-maintained source takes precedence.
